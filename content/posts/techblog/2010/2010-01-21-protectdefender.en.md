**ProtectDefender** is a rogue security product in the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — one more clone from the same rebrand mill that flooded late 2009 ([clones coming thick and fast](/2009/12/18/winiguard-clones-are-coming-thick-and-fast/)).

It presents fake scan results and scare warnings to push a paid unlock. Do not purchase it; remove it with the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).
