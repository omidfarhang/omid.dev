**SysDefenders** is a rogue security product in the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — another early-2010 rebrand in a line that was already spinning out new names every few days by late 2009 ([The biggest rogue family](/2009/12/15/the-biggest-rogue-family/)).

Same playbook: fake infection results, scare warnings, and a paid unlock that does not protect the machine. Do not buy it. If it is installed, follow the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).
