**PcsProtector** is a rogue security product in the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — the first clone of that line spotted in 2010. Same operators, new paint: fake scan results, scare warnings, and a paid unlock that does not protect the machine.

By late 2009 the family was already a revolving door of rebrands ([The biggest rogue family](/2009/12/15/the-biggest-rogue-family/); [clones coming thick and fast](/2009/12/18/winiguard-clones-are-coming-thick-and-fast/)). PcsProtector continues that pattern into the new year.

Do not buy it. If it is installed, remove it with the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/) rather than licensing the scareware.
