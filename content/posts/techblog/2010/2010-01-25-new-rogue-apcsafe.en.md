**APcSafe** is another rogue anti-spyware clone of the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — part of the same revolving-door rebrand pattern documented in [The biggest rogue family](/2009/12/15/the-biggest-rogue-family/).

Fake infection results and scare warnings push a paid unlock that does not protect the machine. Do not buy it. If it is installed, follow the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).
