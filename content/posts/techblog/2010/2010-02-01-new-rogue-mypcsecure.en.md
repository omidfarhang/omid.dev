**MyPcSecure** is a rogue anti-spyware application and a clone from the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — more of the same rebrand conveyor belt into early 2010 ([clones coming thick and fast](/2009/12/18/winiguard-clones-are-coming-thick-and-fast/)).

It shows fake infections and pushes a paid unlock that does not protect the machine. Do not buy it; follow the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).
