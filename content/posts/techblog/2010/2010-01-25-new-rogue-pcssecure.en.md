**PcsSecure** is a cloned rogue antispyware from the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — another 2010 nameplate on the same scareware line that was already producing clones every couple of days in late 2009 ([Like clockwork…](/2009/12/16/like-clockwork-the-next-member-of-the-winiguard-rogue-family-appears/)).

Do not license it. If the PC is infected, remove it with the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).
