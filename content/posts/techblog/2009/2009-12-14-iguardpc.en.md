**IGuardPC** is a rogue security product in the [WiniGuard](/2009/01/12/winiguard/) family — one of the many rebrands that appeared in late 2009. Contemporary notes treated it as roughly the **50th** clone in that line ([The biggest rogue family](/2009/12/15/the-biggest-rogue-family/)).

Like other family members, it presents fake infection results and pushes a paid unlock. Do not purchase it; follow the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/).

## Known System Changes

### Files

- `%CommonDesktop%\IGuardPc.lnk`
- `%ProgramFiles%\IGuardPc Software\IGuardPc\IGuardPc.exe`

### Folders

- `%CommonPrograms%\IGuardPc`
- `%ProgramFiles%\IGuardPc Software`

### Registry Entries

- Key: `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\IGuardPc`
- Key: `HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Software\IGuardPc`
- Key: `HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Uninstall\IGuardPc`
- Key: `HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run`
- Value: `IGuardPc.exe`
