**Advanced Virus Remover** is rogue security software. Do not judge it by the title — the name is marketing for a scam.

## How it spreads

It typically reaches PCs via Trojans and misleading ads. Once installed it shows warnings, alerts, and scan results claiming the PC is infected. Those warnings are false, designed to scare people into buying a “full version” that does not clean the system.

## What to do

Do not buy Advanced Virus Remover. Follow the [Malware Removal guide for Windows](/2011/01/02/malware-removal-guide-for-Windows/). Related branding abuse from the same week: [Rebranded rogue claims to be McAfee Secure certified](/2009/12/11/rebranded-rogue-claims-to-be-mcafee-secure-certified/).
