Chat moves an idea quickly, but it is not where I trust it to live. [Git-tracked Markdown](/2026/08/29/one-ai-chat-is-not-a-research-workspace/) is the durable layer; [Jupyter](/2025/12/23/jupyter-the-strategic-value-of-thinking-in-notebooks/) is where an idea becomes executable; [Obsidian](/2026/09/22/obsidian-on-a-git-research-vault/) is an optional way to browse the same files. The important part is not choosing one surface — it is keeping the handoffs visible.
